Privacy Policy – Patang Clash

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Patang Clash (“the Game”, “we”, “us”), published by Hassan Talha, handles information. We built the Game to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we have no user accounts or servers. Your game progress (coins, kites, scores, settings) is saved only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS – only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address, which can indicate your approximate (city-level) location
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks) and basic device / app diagnostic information

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • EEA / UK / Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iOS: the Game asks permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using your IDFA. If you decline, ads are not personalised with it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
  • You can buy Remove Ads in the Game to stop banners and pop-up ads.

3. Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the Game is played and to fix bugs, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They may automatically collect:

  • Gameplay events, such as games started and finished, score, time played, how a game ended, kites unlocked, upgrades bought, rewards claimed and rewarded ads watched
  • A random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate (country/city-level) location derived from IP address
  • Crash reports: the technical details of an error, device state and the time it happened

This data is not used to identify you personally and is not sold. Learn more: Firebase privacy and security.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card or billing details. We only receive confirmation of which item was purchased so we can deliver it.

6. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or use Settings → Reset progress. Data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is governed by Google’s retention policies (Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months); you can control ad data with the choices in section 2.

8. Security

All network communication by the advertising, analytics and store SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we don’t hold personal data on servers, most requests are handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You can still contact us anytime.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with a new effective date.
