Questions & answers

Is Patang Clash free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases such as coin packs and Remove Ads. You never need to pay to enjoy the game.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can play fully offline. An internet connection is only used for ads, purchases and anonymous crash reports.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Shop in the game and buy Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack, which includes it). Banner and pop-up ads stop; optional reward videos stay available if you want bonus coins.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?

Sign in to the same Google account, open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads comes back automatically. Coins and game progress are saved on your device only, so they don't move to a new phone.

How do I delete my data?

Patang Clash has no accounts and no servers – your progress is stored only on your phone. To delete data:

  1. Game progress: in the game, open Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the game.
  2. Advertising ID: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads and tap Delete advertising ID.
  3. Anything else: e-mail hassantalha807@gmail.com and we will help within 30 days.

Anonymous analytics data from Google Firebase is deleted automatically after at most 14 months. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

Patang Clash runs on Android phones and tablets. An iPhone version may follow later.

🪁 Fly safe in real life

Patang Clash is a game. When you fly real kites, stay far away from electric wires and never use glass-coated or metal string (dangerous “manja”) – it hurts people and birds and is illegal in many places.
