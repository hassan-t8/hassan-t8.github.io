How to play

Easy to learn, hard to master

Every round takes a minute or two. Ruling the sky takes a little longer.

One finger is all you need

No buttons, no joystick. Touch anywhere on the screen to fly.

1

Hold to rise

Keep your finger on the screen and your kite climbs into the wind.

2

Release to drop

Let go and your kite dips down. Mix short holds to stay at the height you want.

3

Slide to steer

Move your finger left or right to guide your patang across the sky.

4

Grab coins

Fly through coin trails and power-ups to fill your pockets.

Win the string fight ✂️

This is the heart of Patang Clash – just like real rooftop pench on Basant.

  • 🪢
    Cross stringsFly your manja across a rival kite's string and a fight begins.
  • 💪
    Hold to pullA tug bar appears. Keep holding to pull your string and fill your side to win. Let go and the rival pulls back!
  • 🎉
    Bo Kata!Their kite is cut loose and drifts away. You earn points and the combo starts.
  • ⚔️
    Get strongerUpgrade Sharp Manja to win fights faster, or grab Glass Manja for instant cuts.

What can bring you down

Your flight ends if any of these get you – so stay sharp!

✂️

Losing a fight

If a rival wins the tug-of-war, your string gets cut. Boss kites pull hard!

🐦

Birds

Flocks fly across the sky. Watch their path and steer around them.

⚡

Electric wires

The wires above the rooftops are deadly. Don't let your kite fly too low.

🛡️ Tip: while a Shield is active, birds and wires can't hurt you and rivals can't cut your string.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Patang Clash free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases such as coin packs and Remove Ads. You never need to pay to enjoy the game.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can play fully offline. An internet connection is only used for ads, purchases and anonymous crash reports.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Shop in the game and buy Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack, which includes it). Banner and pop-up ads stop; optional reward videos stay available if you want bonus coins.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?

Sign in to the same Google account, open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads comes back automatically. Coins and game progress are saved on your device only, so they don't move to a new phone.

I paid for coins but didn't get them.

Close the game completely and open it again – unfinished purchases are completed automatically when the game starts. If the coins still don't appear, e-mail us your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") and we'll help.

How do I delete my data?

Patang Clash has no accounts and no servers – your progress is stored only on your phone. To delete data:

  1. Game progress: in the game, open Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the game.
  2. Advertising ID: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads and tap Delete advertising ID.
  3. Anything else: e-mail hassantalha807@gmail.com and we will help within 30 days.

Anonymous analytics data from Google Firebase is deleted automatically after at most 14 months. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

Patang Clash runs on Android phones and tablets. An iPhone version may follow later.

🪁 Fly safe in real life

Patang Clash is a game. When you fly real kites, stay far away from electric wires and never use glass-coated or metal string (dangerous “manja”) – it hurts people and birds and is illegal in many places.
